## 60-Second Tutorial Script

1. Open the Actor and keep the default `RELIANCE` input.
2. Keep both sources selected, set the tolerance to 2%, and require a complete comparison.
3. Open the dataset and show the agreement score, discrepancies, and side-by-side values.
4. Point out a real metric where the two sources differ and show both source URLs.
5. Export the comparison to CSV or JSON, or copy the Apify API endpoint for a data-quality workflow.

## Short Post Copy

Moneycontrol and Screener.in do not always show the same Indian stock numbers. I built an Apify Actor that compares both in one row, calculates percentage differences, flags mismatches, and gives an agreement score. One RELIANCE test immediately highlighted which metrics matched and which needed review.

## Positioning Guard

Promote it as a public-data comparison and quality-control tool. Do not claim an agreement proves correctness, and do not position it as financial advice, trading recommendations, guaranteed real-time data, or a replacement for licensed market data feeds. Link users who want live NSE/BSE indices and long financial history to the separate NSE/BSE Actor.
